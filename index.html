📖 Tutorial

Deploy in 5 Steps

Follow along step by step. Each one is simple — and you'll have a live site at the end!

  1. 1
    👤

    Create a Netlify Account

    Head to netlify.com and click Sign Up. You can use your GitHub account, GitLab, or just an email address. It's completely free — no payment info required.

    Once you verify your email, you'll land on your personal Netlify dashboard. That's home base for all your sites.

  2. 2
    📁

    Prepare Your Website Files

    Make sure all your website files are inside a single folder on your computer. Netlify will upload the whole folder at once.

    my-website/ ├── index.html ← Required! Your main page ├── style.css ← Optional: your styles └── script.js ← Optional: your scripts

    ⚠️ Important: Your folder must contain a file named index.html — this is the file browsers load first.

  3. 3
    🖥️

    Open the Netlify Dashboard

    After logging in, you'll see your Netlify dashboard. Scroll down until you see a big drop zone that reads:

    Want to deploy a new site without connecting to Git? Drag and drop your site output folder here

    That's where the magic happens. Keep this tab open and find your folder.

  4. 4
    🖱️

    Drag & Drop Your Folder

    Open your file manager (Finder on Mac, File Explorer on Windows), find your website folder, then drag the entire folder onto the Netlify dashboard drop zone.

    Netlify will show a progress bar and start uploading your files. This usually takes just a few seconds!

    💡 Tip: Make sure you're dragging the folder, not just the individual files inside it.

  5. 5
    🎉

    Your Website Is Live!

    As soon as the upload finishes, Netlify gives you a unique URL for your site. It looks something like this:

    🌐 amazing-site-abc123.netlify.app

    Click the link and your website will open in the browser — live on the real internet! Share it with anyone in the world. 🌍

    To update your site, simply drag your folder again. Netlify keeps all your previous versions too.

⚠️ Watch Out

Common Beginner Mistakes

Don't panic if something goes wrong. These are the most common slip-ups — and how to fix them fast.

Netlify needs a file called index.html (lowercase, exact spelling) in the root of your folder. This is the file that browsers open first. If it's missing or misnamed (like Index.HTML or homepage.html), visitors will see a "Page Not Found" error. Rename your main file to exactly index.html and re-upload.
If your CSS or images aren't loading, check your file paths in HTML. Use relative paths like ./style.css or images/photo.jpg — never absolute paths like C:\Users\You\Documents\style.css. Those only work on your own computer and will break on any server.
Drag the folder that contains your files — not a parent folder or just individual files. For example, if your project is in Desktop/my-website/, drag the my-website folder. If you drag a parent folder by accident, Netlify might not find your index.html in the expected location.
Your CSS file is uploaded but the styles aren't showing? Check that you've linked it properly in your HTML. Inside your <head> tag you need: <link rel="stylesheet" href="style.css">. Make sure the filename matches exactly — including capitalisation.
